Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026 • GDPR & CCPA Compliant

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the mobile application Luys: Armenian Bible & AI ("Application", "we", "us") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users.

1. Personal Data & Privacy Commitment

Luys: Armenian Bible & AI is built with user privacy as a cornerstone. We do NOT collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location).

2. Local Storage & Offline Functionality

All core spiritual content, prayer bookmarks, reading history, and application settings are stored strictly on your local device and are never transmitted to external cloud servers without your explicit action.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To support the development and maintenance of the free version of the Application, third-party advertising networks are utilized. These networks may collect non-personally identifiable technical identifiers (such as device model, OS version, IP address, and advertising identifiers IDFA/IDFV where permitted) strictly to deliver relevant advertisements and prevent ad fraud.

The Application may utilize the following accredited partner networks:

  • VK Ad Network / myTarget (VK LLC) — provides mobile advertising and mediation. Privacy Policy: https://legal.vk.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — provides global mobile app advertising. Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Ad-Free Experience: Users who subscribe to or purchase Luys Premium enjoy an entirely ad-free experience, and all advertising SDKs are disabled.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In compliance with Apple iOS 14.5+ guidelines, the Application requests explicit user permission before any tracking identifier is accessed. Users may grant or revoke this permission at any time via iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: samjan190799@gmail.com
